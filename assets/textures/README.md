# Racing asphalt

`racing-asphalt.png` is the road's diffuse texture, generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 2026-09-19. It is bundled with the game. World-space tiling keeps corners and the lap seam continuous; linear-light mipmaps and anisotropic filtering keep the fine aggregate stable at distance. Painted lines and kerbs use their original colours.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless square diffuse/albedo texture tile for a 3D racing game's asphalt road. Generate a production-ready 1024x1024 tile of dry, high-quality modern racing circuit tarmac, viewed perfectly orthographically straight down, filling every pixel. Fine densely compacted dark mineral aggregate in asphalt binder, tiny irregular gray stone flecks, understated mottling and very subtle road wear. Mid-dark neutral charcoal gray with a slight warm tone, low contrast so repeated tiling looks natural. The tile represents about one square metre of real asphalt; grains are very fine, not gravel boulders. Entire image is material only. Flat diffuse illumination, absolutely no directional shadows, no glare, no vignette or gradient, no perspective. Seamlessly tileable on all four edges; avoid conspicuous individual patches that repeat. No road lines, no painted markings, no tyre marks, no cracks, no potholes, no debris, no water, no text, no borders. A physically plausible restrained race-track tarmac surface rather than a stylized pattern.
