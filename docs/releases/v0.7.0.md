Todora 0.7.0 brings the #95 Aston Martin V8 Vantage GTE to the garage, adds wheel sounds and quiet Omarchy radio, and gives you controls to reset saved ghosts.

## New since 0.6.0

- **VANTAGE #95** replaces the default estate car with a Blender-built recreation of DHH, Kristian Poulsen and Nicki Thiim’s 2014 Le Mans GTE Am class winner. It includes the pale-blue and orange livery, yellow endurance lights, animated wheels, rear wing and diffuser. Clubman and Express use the same model with their own colours and handling.
- **Revised proportions and handling:** the car is about 1.06 game units long and 0.45 across the body, with matching ghost and wheel contacts. Aerodynamic grip contribution is doubled. Acceleration builds more gradually: the default Vantage reaches displayed 200 km/h in about five seconds on flat road.
- **Wheel-only driving sound:** quiet rolling contact continues while moving or coasting, with rubber squeal building near and beyond the grip limit. Wheels go quiet at rest and on pause. There is no motor sound.
- **Omarchy radio** streams quietly in the background. **M** toggles music; **F8** toggles all sound. Music continues through menus, and sound fades when the window loses focus. Wheel sounds work offline.
- **Reset saved ghosts from the pause menu:** press **Esc**, choose **Reset this ghost** or **Reset all ghosts**, then click the same button again to confirm. This clears the corresponding best times and restarts the lap. “This ghost” applies to the current circuit and driving mode.
- **Clean quitting:** **Cmd+Q** on macOS, **Ctrl+Q** elsewhere, or **Quit game** in the pause menu stops audio and signals radio shutdown. Native macOS Quit and window closing also clean up audio.

All forty circuits retain the wider, shorter layouts from 0.6.0. Existing ghosts remain available until you reset them.

## Install

Download **Todora.dmg**, open it, and drag Todora to Applications. This build is for **Apple Silicon Macs**.

The app is signed ad hoc and is not notarized. macOS may require permission in **System Settings → Privacy & Security** before the first launch.

With **SHA256SUMS** beside the disk image, verify the download using `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS`.

To build from source, check out `v0.7.0` and run `tools/package_macos.sh`. The repository includes the editable Blender model, asset generators and tyre audio attribution; radio music is streamed rather than bundled.
