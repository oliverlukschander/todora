Todora 0.8.0 corrects the racing directions at Paul Ricard and Marina Bay, adds continuous grass around every circuit, and improves menu controls.

## Changes since 0.7.0

- **All 40 racing directions checked:** Paul Ricard now runs clockwise and Marina Bay anticlockwise. Buenos Aires already ran correctly for its represented layout. The track importer now preserves verified racing order when regenerating circuits. [Audit and sources](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/blob/v0.8.0/docs/track-screening/directions.md).
- **Continuous grass terrain:** infields follow the road edges, and each course sits on a rectangle extending at least 50 metres beyond its outermost verge on every side. Suzuka's bridge ground and unwanted dark shadow are fixed.
- **Racing asphalt:** a fine-grained road texture stays stable at distance while painted lines and kerbs remain crisp.
- **Keyboard and gamepad menus:** arrows, D-pad and left stick navigate the start and pause menus; Enter or A activates the selection. The D-pad also matches arrow-key driving controls. The mouse cursor appears in menus and hides while driving.
- **Independent audio controls:** M toggles Omarchy music; F8 toggles tyre sounds. Both are available in the pause menu. Driving audio remains wheel-only.
- **Linux packaging:** a build script packages the game and assets with a desktop launcher and optional local installation.

Ghosts recorded on the old reversed Paul Ricard and Marina Bay layouts are no longer loaded. Records on the other circuits remain compatible.

## Install

Download **Todora.dmg**, open it, and drag Todora to Applications. This build is for **Apple Silicon Macs**. The app is signed ad hoc and is not notarized; macOS may require permission in **System Settings → Privacy & Security** before the first launch.

With **SHA256SUMS** beside the disk image, verify it using `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS`.

To build from source, check out `v0.8.0` and run `tools/package_macos.sh`, or `tools/package_linux.sh --install` on Linux. The downloadable app was built and checked on macOS; no Linux binary is included in this release.

## Validation

178 Rust tests and 4 importer tests pass, including racing direction, complete driving laps and terrain coverage across all 40 circuits. Eight opt-in diagnostic tests remain ignored. Formatting and Clippy pass. The packaged app launches on macOS, quits with audio shutdown confirmed, and passes signature and bundled-asset checks.
