Todora 0.11.0 beta 1 adds Game Center shared practice for two Macs. Both drivers share the same circuit and driving mode, see each other’s cars, and compare valid lap times. Driving physics stay local; cars do not collide.

This is a development-signed test build for registered Apple Silicon Macs. Your Mac must be included in the provisioning profile before downloading; sending the DMG to an unregistered Mac is not enough. Live Game Center matchmaking still needs a two-player test.

1. Download **Todora-Multiplayer.dmg**, open it, and drag **Todora Multiplayer** to Applications.
2. Both players must install this same beta and sign into different Game Center accounts.
3. Choose a circuit and driving mode, then press **M** or click **Multiplayer** to invite your friend or find a match. One player’s circuit and mode will be selected for both.
4. Press **M** again to cancel or leave. **R** resets your car. **Esc** pauses your controls but the shared session continues, invalidating your current lap.

The remote car is cyan and your saved ghost is amber. Music now toggles with **N**; the garage still uses **M** to select driving mode. The controller’s right-stick click also opens or leaves multiplayer.

The app is development-signed, not notarized. If macOS blocks it, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. A missing device registration cannot be fixed by this override. With **SHA256SUMS** beside the disk image, verify it using `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS`.

Linux remains a single-player platform; Apple features are optional. Windows remains untested. This beta does not replace the stable 0.10.0 release.

Validation: 205 Rust tests passed with eight optional reports ignored; formatting, all-feature Clippy, and signing-profile checks passed. Linux CI must pass for the release commit before publication. Live Apple sign-in and two-Mac matchmaking remain the purpose of this beta.
