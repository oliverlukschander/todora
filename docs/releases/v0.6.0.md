Todora 0.6.0 gives the cars more room and makes every circuit a shorter lap. It also adds three speed modes and updates the corner markers and gamepad controls.

## New since 0.5.0

- **Cars are 25% smaller**, with matching wheel contacts, wheelbase and ghost models. Steering is adjusted to preserve the previous low-speed turning radius.
- **Every road is 3.6 m wide**, up from 3.3 m, with 2.94 m of asphalt. Runoff and corner plaques fit the smaller cars.
- **All 40 circuits are shorter.** Thirty-five lose about 10% of their lap length; Baku, Jeddah, Miami, Suzuka and Zandvoort have smaller reductions to keep nearby road sections clear. Laps range from 446 m at Magny-Cours to 1,807 m at Baku. The circuit menu shows the new lengths.
- **Beginner, Regular and Pro speed modes** offer 80%, 100% and 120% of regular speed. Choose a mode in the car menu with M or the gamepad's top face button, then apply it. Each circuit and mode has its own saved ghost.
- **Three flat chevrons mark approaching corners** on the outside verge, replacing the standing diamonds.
- **Gamepad bumpers switch menu tabs.** The right bumper also resets the car while driving.
- The starting run-up is shortened with the tracks, and the Clubman gets a small top-speed adjustment to keep the three cars competitive.

## Existing lap records

The layouts have changed, so ghosts saved on the previous surfaces are no longer loaded. New records use the rebuilt tracks; incompatible old files are not used as times to beat.

## Install

Download **Todora.dmg**, open it, and drag Todora to Applications. This build is for **Apple Silicon Macs**.

The app is signed ad hoc and is not notarized. macOS may require permission in **System Settings → Privacy & Security** before the first launch.

**SHA256SUMS** contains the disk image's checksum. With both files in the same folder, verify it using `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS`.

To build from source, check out `v0.6.0` and run `tools/package_macos.sh`; the build uses the committed dependency lockfile.
