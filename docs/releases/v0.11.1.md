Todora 0.11.1 puts you in the driver's seat: a full cockpit view, a bonnet camera that really sits on the bonnet, WASD in every menu, Omarchy boards along the straights and a frame-rate readout you can see.

- **Three camera views**, on **V** or the D-pad's up:
  - **Chase**, zoomed with the scroll wheel as before. The fixed close chase is folded into the zoom.
  - **Bonnet**, now just above the bonnet between the front wheels, with the nose along the bottom of the frame. It used to float above the roof.
  - **Cockpit**, new: from the driver's seat, with a carbon dashboard, a display, shift lights and switches, a race steering wheel that turns as you steer, A-pillars, a roof liner and a roll cage, looking out over the bonnet.
  Both on-board views pitch and roll with the car.
- **WASD in every menu:** the title, pause menu, circuit list, garage, settings, leaderboard and replay move with W A S D as well as the arrows. In the circuit list they browse until you type another letter; **/** first starts a search that begins with one of them. On the name field in Settings they are letters. The weekly challenge's "drive it" is now **F**. A key still held from driving no longer nudges a menu that has just opened.
- **Omarchy on the trackside boards:** half the advertising boards carry Omarchy's logo mark and wordmark, drawn cell for cell from the official files, in its green on Tokyo Night.
- **Frame-rate readout:** **Settings → Display → Show frame rate** now shows a panel of its own under the circuit's name. It was drawn as faint grey text over the sky before.

**Saves:** a settings file that chose the close chase opens on the chase. Best times, ghosts, medals and online laps are unchanged, and 0.11.1 uses the same world leaderboards as 0.11.0.

**Downloads**

- **Todora.dmg** — the game for **Apple Silicon Macs**, signed ad hoc and not notarized. Open it and drag Todora to Applications; macOS may ask for permission in **System Settings → Privacy & Security** on first launch.
- **Todora-Multiplayer.dmg** — the same game with Game Center shared practice, development-signed for the Macs registered in its provisioning profile only. Both players need this same version.
- **Todora-linux-x86_64.tar.gz** — for x86-64 Linux, built on Debian bookworm (glibc 2.36). Unpack it and run `Todora/todora`. It needs ALSA, udev, Wayland or X11 and a Vulkan driver.
- **SHA256SUMS** — verify the downloads with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS`.
