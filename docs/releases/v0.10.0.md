Todora 0.10.0 adds a circular mini-map, mini-sector timing and more freedom to leave the road, with gentler grass resistance and a subtle rubbered racing line across all 40 circuits.

- **Circular mini-map:** the player stays near the centre with their heading upward and roughly 65 metres of road visible ahead. The map scales with the HUD and distinguishes Suzuka’s bridge from the road underneath.
- **Mini-sector timing:** each circuit has 4–8 sectors, with more on longer tracks. Completed sectors briefly show their difference from the corresponding sector of your best valid lap for that circuit and driving mode: green for faster, red for slower. Without a reference, they show the sector time.
- **Driveable runoff and track limits:** explore the surrounding grass beyond the old verge boundary. A lap becomes invalid when all four tyres leave asphalt and kerbs. “LAP INVALID” stays visible after rejoining while the clock continues; invalid laps cannot replace best times, sector references or ghosts. Recovery remains available at the terrain boundary or when stuck, including beneath Suzuka’s bridge.
- **Gentler grass resistance:** the additional off-track resistance is 25% weaker. Grass retains its lower grip, while asphalt handling and speed settings remain unchanged.
- **Rubbered racing paths:** a soft, lightly varied dark band follows smooth outside–apex–outside paths. It stays within the asphalt, follows elevation and bridge geometry, and preserves road markings and shadow-free lighting.

**Ghost compatibility:** older recordings did not enforce these track limits, so their ghosts and best times are no longer loaded. New valid laps establish references for this version. Existing files remain until replaced by a new valid best.

Download **Todora.dmg**, open it, and drag Todora to Applications. This build is for **Apple Silicon Macs**, signed ad hoc and not notarized. macOS may require permission in **System Settings → Privacy & Security** on first launch. With **SHA256SUMS** beside the disk image, verify it using `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS`.

To build from source, check out `v0.10.0` and run `tools/package_macos.sh`, or `tools/package_linux.sh --install` on Linux. No Linux binary is included.

Validation: 193 Rust tests and four importer tests pass, with eight optional reports ignored; formatting and Clippy pass. Visual checks cover Monza, Spa, both levels of Suzuka and an 800×600 HUD. The packaged app passes signature and startup checks. These scripted checks do not replace a hands-on keyboard/gamepad session for judging feel.
