Todora 0.11.0 is the biggest update yet: world leaderboards that drive every lap again before it counts, a 3-2-1-GO start, medals, fifty achievements, a weekly challenge, replays and a photo mode, a full settings page, five languages, and a car and forty circuits that are Todora's own.

- **Todora's own car and circuits:** the default car is now the **OMARCHY GT #95**, an invented endurance GT in the pale blue and orange DHH raced in, with OMARCHY RACING on the wing and DHH on the plate. Every circuit has an invented name that nods to where it is — Parco Reale, Ardennes Loop, Figure-Eight Hills, Styrian Bowl — and the circuit search still finds one by the place it is modelled on. Layouts, saved laps and best times are unchanged.
- **World leaderboards** at todora.lukschander.com, opt-in: your best laps are uploaded signed, driven again on the server by the game's own physics, and only then placed. World, country, rivals and weekly boards, a records view with every circuit's best, medal and world place, and downloadable ghosts to race beside your own. No account, email or address; **Settings → Online** renames you or deletes everything. See the [privacy note](../privacy.md).
- **Start lights:** a 3-2-1-GO countdown holds the car on the grid, in full on arrival and short after a restart, or off.
- **Timing:** sectors turn purple, green or yellow on a sector bar, a lap card sums up each lap (sectors, top speed, slowest corner, medal gap), and a new best gets a banner, a flashing clock and a chime.
- **Medals and achievements:** bronze, silver, gold and author times on every circuit and mode, and fifty achievements announced as you earn them.
- **Weekly challenge:** one circuit a week for everyone, with its own board.
- **Replay and photo mode:** replay your best, your last lap or a downloaded ghost from trackside, chase or bonnet cameras, at any speed, and save a screenshot.
- **Settings:** audio, display (full screen, vsync, anti-aliasing, frame limit, render scale that really draws fewer pixels, HUD size, TV-safe margin, field of view, frame-rate readout), HUD, controls with rebindable keys and pad buttons, and accessibility: colour-blind colours, high contrast, reduced motion, tap-to-hold pedals and Beginner driving assists.
- **More to see and hear:** a title screen, drifting clouds, braking boards, a chequered line, grandstands, pits, marshals and tyre walls, an engine note, and pad rumble for grass, kerbs, grip and the big moments. Three camera views: chase, close chase and bonnet.
- **Languages:** English, German, French, Spanish and Italian, following the system or chosen in the HUD settings.
- **Game Center shared practice** from the 0.11.0 beta is included in the separate multiplayer build.

**Saves:** settings move to version 2 of `settings.json`, and a version-1 file is upgraded. Best times and ghosts carry over. Laps are now recorded bit-identically from their inputs on macOS and Linux, which is what the leaderboards check.

**Downloads**

- **Todora.dmg** — the game for **Apple Silicon Macs**, signed ad hoc and not notarized. Open it and drag Todora to Applications; macOS may ask for permission in **System Settings → Privacy & Security** on first launch.
- **Todora-Multiplayer.dmg** — the same game with Game Center shared practice, development-signed for the Macs registered in its provisioning profile only. It does not run on other Macs.
- **Todora-linux-x86_64.tar.gz** — for x86-64 Linux, built on Debian bookworm (glibc 2.36). Unpack it and run `Todora/todora`; the assets sit beside it. It needs ALSA, udev, Wayland or X11 and a Vulkan driver. From source, `tools/package_linux.sh --install` also adds a launcher entry.
- **SHA256SUMS** — verify the downloads with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS`.

Validation: 317 game tests and 15 server tests pass, with twelve optional reports ignored; formatting and Clippy (default, all features, server) pass. A test sets up every system of the whole game, and the game is launched for real before every commit. Visual checks cover the title, circuit menu, garage, leaderboard, settings, lap card, countdown, replay and render scale, in English, German and French. Lap replays agree bit for bit on macOS arm64 and Linux arm64 and amd64. These scripted checks do not replace a hands-on keyboard and gamepad session for judging feel.
