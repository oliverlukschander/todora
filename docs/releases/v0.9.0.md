Todora 0.9.0 brings natural grass, clearer hills and a rebuilt Suzuka bridge, with a deeper, muffled tyre rumble.

## Changes since 0.8.0

- **Natural grass:** verges and surrounding terrain share a detailed grass texture that stays stable at distance.
- **Clearer lighting:** shadows are disabled and circuit surfaces stay evenly lit, including Spa's steep hills and Suzuka's underpass.
- **Fitted terrain:** the surrounding fill meets the track at road height. Ground beneath Suzuka's crossing follows the lower road, with smoother transitions into the bridge approaches.
- **Obsidian Suzuka bridge:** a dark deck, slim graphite rails, underside girders and fitted supports give the crossing a finished structure. Retaining walls and closed approach ends fill the gaps while keeping both roads clear.
- **Muffled rolling sound:** steady tyre contact is now a deep bass rumble with the higher frequencies filtered out. The cornering scrub and squeal are unchanged.

The changed verge profile means ghosts saved in earlier versions are no longer loaded. New laps establish records for the updated track surfaces.

## Install

Download **Todora.dmg**, open it, and drag Todora to Applications. This build is for **Apple Silicon Macs**. The app is signed ad hoc and is not notarized; macOS may require permission in **System Settings → Privacy & Security** before the first launch.

With **SHA256SUMS** beside the disk image, verify it using `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS`.

To build from source, check out `v0.9.0` and run `tools/package_macos.sh`, or `tools/package_linux.sh --install` on Linux. No Linux binary is included.

## Validation

179 Rust tests and 4 importer tests pass, including terrain coverage across all 40 circuits and Suzuka bridge clearance. Eight opt-in tests remain ignored. Formatting and Clippy pass. An audio comparison confirms that the cornering scrub and squeal are unchanged.
