**51 circuits. Your name and flag on the grid. Up to four players on one screen.** Todora is a free arcade racer for Apple Silicon Macs and Linux: learn the circuits, race a ghost and chase purple sectors.

**Downloads**

- **[Todora.dmg](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.11.2/Todora.dmg)** — for **Apple Silicon Macs**. Open the image and drag Todora to Applications. Signed ad hoc, not notarized; first launch may require permission in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- **[Todora-linux-x86_64.tar.gz](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.11.2/Todora-linux-x86_64.tar.gz)** — for **x86-64 Linux**, built on Debian bookworm (glibc 2.36). Unpack and run `Todora/todora`. Requires ALSA, udev, Wayland or X11, and a Vulkan driver.
- **[Todora-Multiplayer.dmg](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.11.2/Todora-Multiplayer.dmg)** — the separate **Game Center development build**, only for Macs registered in its provisioning profile. Both players need 0.11.2. Local split-screen is included in the regular downloads above and does not need this build.
- **[SHA256SUMS](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.11.2/SHA256SUMS)** — download alongside the packages and check with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS`.

**What changed in 0.11.2**

- **Eleven more circuits:** Le Mans, Fuji, Sebring, Laguna Seca, Lime Rock, Long Beach, Mid-Ohio, Mosport, Road America, Road Atlanta and VIR inspire the new courses. Find all 51 under **All**, **DHH ’14**, **Endurance**, **Grand Prix** and **Heritage**, or search by the Todora name, real venue or common abbreviation. A loading screen shows which circuit is preparing.
- **Local split-screen for 2–4 players:** join from the starting screen with two keyboard layouts and individually assigned controllers. Each driver gets a car, camera and lap clock; a disconnected controller pauses everyone. Local session times stay separate from personal records and world rankings. [Controls](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/blob/v0.11.2/docs/local-play.md).
- **Your driver profile:** choose a custom name and optional country flag, then save it to the online service. Names such as DHH and published Omarchy team handles are reserved. The leaderboard has World, Country and Rivals views, with clearer profile and connection feedback.
- **An easier ghost race:** select a driver and choose **Race this ghost**. Todora downloads and verifies the lap, loads the right circuit and starts the race. **Find a close rival** suggests a nearby faster time where one is available.
- **71 achievements:** new circuit passports and gold collections reflect the expanded catalogue. Existing awards and earned dates are retained.
- **Recorded engine audio:** three blended layers respond to RPM and throttle, with smoother gear changes and a softer note on overrun. Engine volume remains separate in **Settings → Audio**.
- **Fixes and polish:** tyre barriers sit correctly beside steep banks on Between the Lakes; settings and achievements save reliably on normal exit; the frame-rate panel respects menu visibility; the starting screen includes the creator credit.

**Your progress**

Existing best laps, ghosts and earned awards are retained. The original 40-circuit achievement thresholds and weekly-challenge rotation stay unchanged. Version 0.11.2 uses the current world leaderboard service; submitted laps are replayed by the server before ranking. Official-build-only admission and separate Workshop rankings are not part of this release.

Game Center shared practice remains a development feature. Live matchmaking between two Macs still needs a hands-on check; automated and local checks do not establish Internet play or controller feel.

Validation: 349 game tests, 17 server tests and 8 Python tooling tests passed, along with formatting and all-feature workspace Clippy. The 0.11.2 client also passed a complete profile/upload/ghost-download round trip against the deployed 0.11.1 server image in a local test environment.
