**51 circuits. Your name and flag on the grid. Up to four players on one screen. And now a chicken.** Todora is a free arcade racer for Apple Silicon Macs and Linux: learn the circuits, race a ghost and chase purple sectors, or switch on Bonkers mode and ride something silly over hills, pads and cows.

[Watch the 30-second gameplay trailer](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.12.0/todora-30s-16x9.mp4) · Made by [@olukschander](https://x.com/olukschander)

**Downloads**

- **[Todora.dmg](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.12.0/Todora.dmg)** — for **Apple Silicon Macs**. Open the image and drag Todora to Applications. Signed ad hoc, not notarized; first launch may require permission in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- **[Todora-linux-x86_64.tar.gz](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.12.0/Todora-linux-x86_64.tar.gz)** — for **x86-64 Linux**, built on Debian bookworm (glibc 2.36). Unpack and run `Todora/todora`. Requires ALSA, udev, Wayland or X11, and a Vulkan driver.
- **[Todora-Multiplayer.dmg](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.12.0/Todora-Multiplayer.dmg)** — the separate **Game Center development build**, only for Macs registered in its provisioning profile. Both players need 0.12.0. Local split-screen is included in the regular downloads above and does not need this build.
- **[SHA256SUMS](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.12.0/SHA256SUMS)** — download alongside the packages and check with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS`.

Todora's MIT license, dependency license texts and asset credits are inside both packages.

**What changed in 0.12.0**

- **Bonkers mode:** a second way to play, right under **Drive** on the title screen, or **F9** while driving. Ride a chicken, a rubber duck, a bathtub or a shopping cart round circuits that have grown hills, over boost pads and jump pads, through cows and bowling pins, under neon, to techno, with chaos events along the way. Speed is the plain car's unless you ask for more. **Settings → Bonkers** changes what it is like.
- **The plain game is untouched, and stays honest:** it is the game as shipped, Todora always opens on it, Bonkers is never saved, and the week's challenge is always driven plain. A Bonkers lap is flagged from its first step and never counts: it cannot become a record or go online, and the driving physics the leaderboard verifies are unchanged. Split-screen and shared practice stand Bonkers down.
- **Game Center invitations work (development build):** accepting an invitation used to end the session before it began, and closing the matchmaking window could crash the app. Both are fixed; the window now opens in a window of its own.
- **Far-car smoothness in shared practice (development build):** the other driver's car is drawn from the timestamps its Mac sends, which are now exact and steady at 60 a second, instead of from when each packet arrived. The panel shows the link: how far behind the car is drawn, jitter, and snapshots lost or late.

All 51 circuits, driver profiles, ghost racing, achievements and local split-screen remain available. Existing laps, ghosts and awards are retained, and this update works with the current world leaderboard service.

Validation: the full Linux CI suite (491 tests, Clippy with warnings denied, formatting, Python tooling) passed on the release commit. Bonkers mode's own tests measure that it steers as well as the shipped game, but it has not yet been driven by many people. Game Center invitations were checked live between two Macs; the new far-car playback is covered by a network simulator but has not yet been tried on two Macs.
