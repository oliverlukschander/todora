**51 circuits. Your name and flag on the grid. Up to four players on one screen.** Todora is a free arcade racer for Apple Silicon Macs and Linux: learn the circuits, race a ghost and chase purple sectors.

[Watch the 30-second gameplay trailer](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.11.3/todora-30s-16x9.mp4) · Made by [@olukschander](https://x.com/olukschander)

**Downloads**

- **[Todora.dmg](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.11.3/Todora.dmg)** — for **Apple Silicon Macs**. Open the image and drag Todora to Applications. Signed ad hoc, not notarized; first launch may require permission in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- **[Todora-linux-x86_64.tar.gz](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.11.3/Todora-linux-x86_64.tar.gz)** — for **x86-64 Linux**, built on Debian bookworm (glibc 2.36). Unpack and run `Todora/todora`. Requires ALSA, udev, Wayland or X11, and a Vulkan driver.
- **[Todora-Multiplayer.dmg](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.11.3/Todora-Multiplayer.dmg)** — the separate **Game Center development build**, only for Macs registered in its provisioning profile. Both players need 0.11.3. Local split-screen is included in the regular downloads above and does not need this build.
- **[SHA256SUMS](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.11.3/SHA256SUMS)** — download alongside the packages and check with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS`.
- **[License and credits supplement](https://github.com/oliverlukschander/todora/releases/download/v0.11.3/Todora-licenses.zip)** — Todora's MIT license, dependency license texts and source links, and asset credits. Added after 0.11.3 was published; download alongside the original packages. Future builds include these notices inside the app.

**What changed in 0.11.3**

- **Steadier engine sound at speed:** the recorded layers now hold their pitch while you cruise or reach maximum speed. This fixes the repeating pitch rise and reset inherited from the original acceleration recording.
- **Smoother blending:** all three engine layers stay aligned as RPM changes, removing the beating caused by their drifting apart.

All 51 circuits, driver profiles, ghost racing, achievements and local split-screen remain available. Existing laps, ghosts and awards are retained, and this update works with the current world leaderboard service.

Validation: 17 audio tests passed, including sustained maximum speed for all three cars and checks for pitch drift and loop joins. The sound fix also passed the full Linux CI suite. Mac and Linux packages were checked for startup and bundled audio assets.

Game Center shared practice remains a development feature; live matchmaking between two Macs still needs a hands-on check. Official-build-only admission and separate Workshop rankings are not part of this release.
